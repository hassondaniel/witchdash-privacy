WitchDash: Escape the Witch — Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Write to hassondaniel2@gmail.com and we'll answer as soon as we can.

Common questions

  • How do I play? Use the arrows to run, hide behind houses and walls before they vanish, and tap the donut to throw it at the witch.
  • How do I build a wall? Press the wall button: each press adds one layer of stones. A finished wall hides you for a few seconds.
  • I bought an extra on another device. Open Extras from the main menu and tap Restore purchases.
  • The voice is too much. Settings → Voice → Off.
  • Change the language. Main menu → the flag button at the top right.

For parents

The game is free. Optional extras are one-time purchases behind a grown-ups check. There are no ads and no data is collected. See the privacy policy.
