WitchDash: Escape the Witch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

WitchDash is a game for children, published by Daniel Hasson (Doris). We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-ins.
  • No advertising and no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • Nothing about you or your child is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress, settings, stars and decorations are saved only on the device.
  • Spoken messages are made by the device's own built-in voices; no text or audio leaves the device.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Purchases

  • The whole game is free. Optional one-time extras are sold through Apple's App Store.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see payment or personal details.
  • Purchases and any links out of the game are behind a parental gate (a question for grown-ups).

Children

The game is made for children and is designed so that no personal information is collected from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
